## Pacing

_(scene length, cuts vs. transitions, breathing room — populated by /feedback over time)_

- Edits should "jump": crisp, fast cuts on the beat, aiming for about one visual event per second with short bursts on drops, like @jsewellbeats. Avoid holding one view longer than ~3 s unless it is the talking-head moment. (2026-10-19)

## Pacing & retention (from JAIL feedback, 2026-10-21)

- Retention matters most at the start: 3 changes in the first ~4 s. Example order: macro/arrangement shot → me + a "putting something in" recording → the first element (e.g. hats).
- Every DAW shot must visibly play in time: notes hit the playhead exactly when heard. When I'm placing notes, the whole pattern must be in frame so you see each note land. (2026-10-21)
- Avoid face-cam moments where I'm talking (mouth moving / speaking to someone). The track plays over the footage, so talking looks out of place. Prefer moments where I'm quietly working or nodding to the beat. (2026-10-22)
- Never use face-cam moments where I look into the lens or break the 4th wall. Only use moments where I'm working on the screen. (2026-10-21)
- Never letterbox a screen recording (no black bars). It must fill its panel. (2026-10-21)
- More dynamic movement like @jsewellbeats: camera tracking the playhead on tight piano-roll shots, tilted (5–10°) slowly rotating playlist shots, and a mix of horizontal whips, vertical whips, zoom-blur punches and hard cuts. All moves must carry motion blur. (2026-10-21)
- Plugin cutaways (Ozone, OTT, Soft Clipper) are welcome, but only from real screen recordings of me using them. (2026-10-21)
- Mixer shots are boring on their own: give them a zoom punch on bar downbeats (with zoom blur) plus a slow push.
- Occasional slow zoom in/out (~7%) on screen recordings and macro shots is good. Occasional, not every shot, and never the old constant pulse/drift.

## Transitions

_(preferred / avoid — populated by /feedback over time)_

- Effects should be syncopated with my beats (on kicks, snares and off-beat hats), similar to @jsewellbeats. (2026-10-19)
- Calm it down: no pulse zooms, no drifting/unnatural zooms, no camera shake, no whip blur on cuts. Hold a steady crop per shot. Cuts about once per bar in drops (~1.3s), every 2 bars in breakdowns; fast flicking (~0.6s shots) felt jarring and disengaging. (2026-10-20, Fast Lane v3 feedback, worst around 0:11-0:18)
- Wants the v4 calm format plus a little dynamism: occasional shakes and velocity shifts (quick push-in punch with eased release) on strong kick / bass hits only, used sparingly "here and there" like @jsewellbeats, not on every hit. (2026-10-20, Fast Lane v4 feedback: "closest one so far")
- Face-cam must be zoomed out: face panel on top at ~40% of frame height, wide framing showing the room (like my Glide / Don't Tell Em edits and @jsewellbeats). Never full-frame face close-ups. When face + DAW share the frame, the DAW is the priority but I'm the brand. (2026-10-20)
- Motion like @jsewellbeats: hard cuts most of the time, a short sideways motion-blur whip (1 frame out, 2 frames in) on some cuts, and small sideways/vertical jolts with motion blur on strong kick/808 hits — not zoom punches. (2026-10-20, studied from his "Testing my new kit" reel)
- Jolts on kick/808 hits: only occasionally (big moments, not every second), and they MUST carry real motion blur along the direction of movement (multi-sample streak). A plain shift without blur looks bad. If it can't be done well, leave jolts out. (2026-10-21, replaces 'roughly once a second')
- Outro: no fisheye/glitch finale on a frozen or face-only shot (Fast Lane v6 outro rejected). End clean on the bar line on a face + DAW shot so the reel loops. (2026-10-21, replaces the earlier "keep glitch finales" guess)
- Hook idea from @pandoraxx918: real social-proof notifications (big producers following / liking) stacked in the first second, repeated at the end to loop. Only ever real notifications. (2026-10-21)
