## Structure

| Time | What |

About 9 cuts in 47 s. Holds run from 3 s up to about 20 s.

## Layout

- The same split for the whole video: **face-cam on top at ~42% of the height, DAW below at ~58%**. No full-frame face and no full-frame DAW-only shots.
- Face framing is wide (chest up, headphones, room and warm practical lights: lamp, LED, shelf). She is looking down at the screen, nodding, and only looks at the lens in the hook and at the end.
- DAW: light, pink-themed FL Studio. The piano roll is zoomed so notes are big blocks with readable note names. The playhead is visible and the view scrolls with it.

## Motion and effects

Almost none: plain cuts between views, no zooms, shakes or transitions. Energy comes from the beat itself, the notes moving under the playhead and the hook.

## Why it works (takeaways for @prodbyjlib)

1. **Social-proof hook in the first second.** Famous-producer follows and likes stacked as screenshots. It stops the scroll and frames the beat as "the one the big producers noticed". Only use **real** notifications, never fabricated ones.
2. **Loop the hook at the end** so the reel replays seamlessly, which raises watch time.
3. **Consistent split layout** with the face smaller and wide: the creator is the brand, the DAW is the content.
4. **Readable DAW:** big zoom on the piano roll so viewers can see the notes.
5. **Calm editing works** when the beat and the hook carry it. It's evidence that over-editing is not required.
