Source: OBS screen recording of the reel in Instagram web (reel window x 400–882, y 152–1008 of a 1920x1080 capture), sent by @prodbyjlib on 2026-10-19 as the motion reference. Studied frame by frame at 60 fps on 2026-10-20.

## Layout

- Split: face-cam on top at ~40%, wide framing (headphones, lamp, slatted wall, plant), DAW below. He is small in the frame.
- Some shots are full-frame DAW (playlist, a mixer / plugin close-up, a piano roll with one or two big notes).

## Motion (the bit the user asked to copy)

- **Mostly hard cuts.** Shots are about 1–3 s.
- **Whip transitions on some cuts:** 1–2 frames (at 30 fps) of strong horizontal motion blur, with the outgoing frame smearing out and the incoming one smearing in from the side. There's a bright streak at the edge and no zoom.
- **Jolts on hits:** inside a shot the DAW panel snaps a short distance sideways or vertically with motion blur on a kick or 808 hit, then settles within a few frames. The face panel stays still.
- No pulse zooms, no constant shake.
