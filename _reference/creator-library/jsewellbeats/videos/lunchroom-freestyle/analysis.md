## What our reels don't have (yet)
1. The beat's own drop-outs become visual moments: hard cut to black for the gap, slam back on the next hit.
2. More plugin eye candy (~5 of 13 shots), each an extreme crop of one detail (dial, GR meter, EQ spectrum), with the camera travelling across it.
3. Uneven pacing: a 4 s opening hold, a fast run, then ~3 s holds.
4. Melody first: face + melody roll as the hook.
5. Ends on a mix shot (EQ), not on the face.
